### Opening

Write one sentence or a short paragraph that names the subject and makes the central point, tension, outcome, or question clear.

### Body

Develop the idea with concrete evidence, reasoning, or an operator lesson. Keep paragraphs easy to scan and preserve uncertainty where the evidence is incomplete.

### Close

End with the implication, takeaway, or a specific question that follows naturally from the post.

### Optional hashtags

Use only relevant hashtags that add categorization or audience value. Omit this section when none improve the post.
